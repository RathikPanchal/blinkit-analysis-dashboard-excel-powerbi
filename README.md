# 🛒 Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

**Blinkit Sales Analysis** is an interactive Power BI dashboard project designed to analyze sales performance, customer satisfaction, and outlet-level inventory distribution.

The project uses key performance indicators (KPIs) and interactive visualizations to identify sales trends, understand product performance, compare outlet characteristics, and generate meaningful business insights.

The dashboard helps stakeholders make data-driven decisions and identify opportunities to improve overall business performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Blinkit needs a comprehensive view of its sales performance across different product categories, fat content types, outlet sizes, outlet locations, and outlet types.

Without a centralized dashboard, it can be difficult to:

- Track overall sales performance.
- Identify high-performing and low-performing product categories.
- Understand customer satisfaction through product ratings.
- Compare sales across different outlet locations and sizes.
- Analyze the relationship between outlet establishment and sales performance.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance using key KPIs.
- Understand product-level sales distribution.
- Evaluate the impact of fat content on sales.
- Compare sales performance across outlet sizes and locations.
- Analyze customer ratings and item-level performance.
- Build an interactive dashboard to support business decision-making.

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard focuses on four major KPIs:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| **Total Sales** | Overall revenue generated from all items sold. |
| **Average Sales** | Average revenue per sale. |
| **Number of Items** | Total count of items sold. |
| **Average Rating** | Average customer rating for items sold. |

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard contains the following visualizations:

### 1. Total Sales by Fat Content
- **Chart Type:** Donut Chart
- Analyzes the impact of fat content on total sales.
- Compares Low Fat and Regular product sales.
- Supports comparison of other KPIs across fat content.

### 2. Total Sales by Item Type
- **Chart Type:** Bar Chart
- Identifies the best-performing product categories.
- Compares sales across different item types.
- Helps understand product category contribution.

### 3. Fat Content by Outlet for Total Sales
- **Chart Type:** Stacked Column Chart
- Compares total sales across outlets based on fat content.
- Helps understand product sales distribution across outlets.

### 4. Total Sales by Outlet Establishment
- **Chart Type:** Line Chart
- Analyzes sales trends based on outlet establishment year.
- Helps identify changes in sales performance over time.

### 5. Sales by Outlet Size
- **Chart Type:** Donut Chart
- Compares sales across Small, Medium, and High outlet sizes.
- Identifies the contribution of different outlet sizes.

### 6. Sales by Outlet Location
- **Chart Type:** Funnel Chart
- Compares sales distribution across Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 locations.
- Helps identify location-wise sales performance.

### 7. All Metrics by Outlet Type
- **Chart Type:** Matrix / Table Visual
- Compares Total Sales, Number of Items, Average Sales, Average Rating, and Item Visibility.
- Provides a consolidated view of outlet performance.

The project was developed using the following steps:

3. **Data Connection:** Importing the dataset into Power BI.
4. **Data Cleaning / Quality Check:** Handling inconsistencies and preparing the data.
5. **Data Modeling:** Organizing tables and establishing relationships where required.
6. **Data Processing:** Transforming and preparing data for analysis.
7. **DAX Calculations:** Creating measures for business KPIs.
8. **Dashboard Layout:** Planning the dashboard structure and visual placement.
9. **Charts Development and Formatting:** Creating and formatting visualizations.
10. **Dashboard Development:** Building the interactive Power BI report.
11. **Insights Generation:** Analyzing the results and identifying business insights.
